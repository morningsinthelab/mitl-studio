About MiTL Studio

MiTL Studio is an AI studio that builds and runs live shows, industry media networks, video sales experiences and AI-powered SEO/GEO for businesses. Founded by Keith Bilous and based in Headingley, Manitoba, Canada, we build and run the media businesses sell through, plus the software behind it. Real people stay on camera. AI handles production, guest booking, clips and distribution.

Since August 2023, MiTL Studio has produced Mornings in the Lab live every weekday: 800+ episodes, 300+ guests and 1,000+ hours of LIVE programming. MiTL stands for Mornings in the Lab. MiTL Studio is not affiliated with Mitel or Mitel Workflow Studio.

Mornings in the Lab

We're your morning accountability partner. Mornings in the Lab with Keith, Jon & Friends is MiTL Studio's flagship weekday live morning show: two hours live, Monday to Friday, at 8:00 AM ET, and we've never missed a live day. 800+ episodes, 300+ guests and 1,000+ hours of LIVE programming.

Running this daily live show is how we built and tested the system that now powers our products and the networks we build, including Roofing Authority Network and Mornings Network. See the flagship show case study.

How we work

Real people stay on camera. AI handles production, guest booking, clips and distribution. ConversationOS is the AI engine that builds every episode: titles, scripts, thumbnails, guests and distribution. MiTL Studio runs on AI: every first decision goes through AI before a person signs off.
