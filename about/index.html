About MiTL Studio

MiTL Studio is an AI studio based in Headingley, Manitoba, Canada that builds and runs the media businesses sell through: live shows, industry networks, video sales experiences, and the software behind them. Real people stay on camera. AI handles production, guest booking, clips and distribution.

Where it comes from

Mornings in the Lab has gone live every weekday since August 2023 at 8:00 AM ET. Running a daily live show is how we built and tested the system that now powers our products and the networks we build, including Roofing Authority Network.

Founder

Keith Bilous founded ICUC, grew it to 400+ staff and sold it to Aegis/iProspect in 2011. He hosts Mornings in the Lab with co-host Jon Andersen. Together, Keith and Jon also co-founded Age Strong, a program for men 50+.
