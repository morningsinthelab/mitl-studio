Sales Desk

Sales Desk by MiTL Studio turns a proposal, brief, product or complex service into a buyer-ready video sales experience. Each one is a story shaped around one buyer and one decision: a finished video, a branded page with a single next step, and reporting on what the buyer actually watched.

How do you sell a complex offer with video?

Don't send a longer deck. Turn the offer into a short story for the person who has to decide, show what changes for their team, and give them one clear next step. That's what a Sales Desk is: MiTL Studio develops the story and script from your approved facts, produces the finished video, and launches it on a branded, shareable page. Then Sales Desk Insights shows views, return visits, sections reached, video engagement and calls to action.

How it works

  1. Bring the source. A proposal, brief, product information, website or approved files, plus who the buyer is and what you want them to do next.
  2. Configure it. Pick the package, finished minutes, production treatment, cast, environment and delivery speed.
  3. Review before production. You approve the story, factual claims, commercial terms and direction before finished production starts.
  4. Production and launch. MiTL Studio produces the video and buyer-facing page. Standard targets 48 hours for eligible projects after source material and script approval.
  5. Share and learn. Send it to the buyer and watch Sales Desk Insights.

How do you sell a complex offer with video?

What is Sales Desk?

Sales Desk is a produced, interactive video sales experience built and run by MiTL Studio. It turns your approved source material into a buyer-specific story, finished video, branded web page, clear next step and engagement reporting.

How long does a Sales Desk take?

What can Sales Desk be used for?

Proposal videos, sales follow-up videos after a meeting, B2B product explainers, introducing a service, walking through a technical process, or building a reusable video-led sales library through an ongoing engagement.
