What is GEO?

GEO (generative engine optimization) is the work of making your brand easy for AI answer tools to find, understand and name. When someone asks ChatGPT, Perplexity or Google AI Overviews who does what you do, GEO is how you become part of that answer. It sits on top of SEO: if search engines can't crawl and trust your pages, AI tools can't either.

How the work runs

  1. Crawl and audit. Every page in your sitemap is checked for status codes, canonicals, robots rules, titles, descriptions and broken links.
  2. Fix indexing. Sitemaps, canonicals and internal links are cleaned up so Google can find and index the pages that matter.
  3. Make your facts machine-readable. Organization, Person, Service, FAQ and video schema with consistent IDs across your sites, so search engines and AI tools connect your brands, your people and what you sell.
  4. Open the door to AI. An llms.txt summary of your business, and AI crawler access set the way you want it.
  5. Watch and keep shipping. Google Search Console data and AI answers are checked on a schedule, and fixes keep going live.

SEO & GEO questions

What is GEO (generative engine optimization)?

GEO is the work of making your brand easy for AI answer tools such as ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews to find, understand and name when someone asks about what you do. It builds on SEO: clean, crawlable pages, clear facts and structured data that machines can read.

How is GEO different from SEO?

SEO aims at ranking pages in search results. GEO aims at being named and cited inside AI-generated answers. They share the same foundation, so we run them together: technical SEO, consistent entity facts, structured data and an llms.txt file, plus checks on how AI tools describe you.

What is llms.txt?

llms.txt is a plain-text file at the root of a website that gives AI systems a short, curated summary of who you are and links to your most important pages. MiTL Studio publishes one on its own sites, including mitl.studio/llms.txt.

Which sites does MiTL Studio run this on?

Our own brands first, including Mornings Network, Roofing Authority Network, ConversationOS, Sales Desk and Guest Engine. The same system is what we run for client sites.
