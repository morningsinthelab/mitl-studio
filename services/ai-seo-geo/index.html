What is GEO?

GEO (generative engine optimization) is the work of making your brand easy for AI answer tools to find, understand and name. When someone asks ChatGPT, Perplexity or Google AI Overviews who does what you do, GEO is how you become part of that answer. It sits on top of SEO: if search engines can't crawl and trust your pages, AI tools can't either.

How do you get your business mentioned in ChatGPT?

Short answer: make your business easy for ChatGPT to find, trust and quote. ChatGPT names businesses it can crawl, understand and see described the same way across the web. That comes from clean, indexed pages, one clear description of what you do, direct answers to your buyers' questions, structured data, and mentions on other sites AI tools cite.

  1. See what ChatGPT says about you today. Ask it the questions your buyers ask, and note whether you come up, how you're described and who shows up instead. Our free AI visibility check does this across ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews.
  2. Let AI crawlers in. Make sure robots.txt allows OAI-SearchBot and GPTBot (or the bots you choose), and that your key pages return 200, have self-referencing canonicals and are not set to noindex.
  3. Get indexed in Google and Bing. Submit a clean sitemap, fix "discovered but not indexed" pages with internal links, and ping new pages through IndexNow.
  4. Say who you are in one sentence, everywhere. Use the same quotable description of your business, your name and what you sell on your site, profiles and directories, so AI tools don't mix you up with similar names.
  5. Answer buyer questions on your own pages. Use question-led headings with a direct answer underneath, such as "Who does X for Y?" or "How much does X cost?", so there is a clear passage to quote.
  6. Make your facts machine-readable. Add Organization, Service and FAQ schema and an llms.txt file that summarizes your business and links your most important pages.
  7. Earn mentions on other sites. Guest spots on shows and podcasts, industry directories, partner pages and roundups give AI tools independent sources that confirm what you do.
  8. Re-check and keep shipping. AI answers change. Check them on a schedule alongside Search Console data, and keep fixes going live.

How the work runs

  1. Crawl and audit. Every page in your sitemap is checked for status codes, canonicals, robots rules, titles, descriptions and broken links.
  2. Fix indexing. Sitemaps, canonicals and internal links are cleaned up so Google can find and index the pages that matter.
  3. Make your facts machine-readable. Organization, Person, Service, FAQ and video schema with consistent IDs across your sites, so search engines and AI tools connect your brands, your people and what you sell.
  4. Open the door to AI. An llms.txt summary of your business, and AI crawler access set the way you want it.
  5. Watch and keep shipping. Google Search Console data and AI answers are checked on a schedule, and fixes keep going live.

What is GEO (generative engine optimization)?

How is GEO different from SEO?

SEO aims at ranking pages in search results. GEO aims at being named and cited inside AI-generated answers. They share the same foundation, so we run them together: technical SEO, consistent entity facts, structured data and an llms.txt file, plus checks on how AI tools describe you.

What is llms.txt?

llms.txt is a plain-text file at the root of a website that gives AI systems a short, curated summary of who you are and links to your most important pages. MiTL Studio publishes one on its own sites, including mitl.studio/llms.txt.
