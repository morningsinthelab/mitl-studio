Why an industry network instead of a podcast

  • It's live. Real people on camera, in real time, on a schedule your industry can count on.
  • It compounds. Each episode adds guest, company and answer pages, so the network becomes the place search engines and AI tools look for answers about your industry.
  • It runs on a proven system. The same production system has run Mornings in the Lab live every weekday since August 2023: 800+ episodes and 300+ guests.

Industry Network questions

What is an industry media network?

An industry media network is a live media network for one industry: its own shows, hosts and AI cast, guest and company pages, and an intelligence layer, so the industry's experts, buyers and answers live in one searchable place.

Who builds a live media network for the roofing industry?

MiTL Studio built the live broadcast system behind Roofing Authority Network, the live broadcast network for commercial roofing, home of The Tie-In and The Service Side. MiTL Studio builds Industry Networks for other industries on the same system.

Does MiTL Studio build networks for industries other than roofing?

Yes. MiTL Studio produces the Let's Talk Supply Chain live shows with founder Sarah Barnes-Humphrey and is building the Supply Chain Authority Council (SCAC) with her. The SCAC founding-member waitlist is open, with full launch in January 2027.

What does an Industry Network include?

Show formats, hosts and an AI cast; live production, clips and distribution; guest booking through Guest Engine; and guest, company and answer pages built for Google and AI search.
