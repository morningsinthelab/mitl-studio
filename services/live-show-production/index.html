Live show production questions

What is MiTL Studio Live Show Production?

A done-for-you, white-label live show production service for networks and hosts. You stay on camera; MiTL Studio builds and runs everything behind it: show format, live production, post-production, AI characters, distribution and ROI reporting.

How much does it cost?

Production is $3,000/mo for one show with up to 4 episodes per month. Production+ is $4,997/mo for up to 3 shows and 12 episodes per month in total. There are no setup fees. Additional AI characters are $997 to build plus $47/mo each, and additional shows beyond Production+ are scoped individually.

How long does it take to launch a show?

Four weeks: discovery and strategy in week 1, build and configure in week 2, rehearsal and refinement in week 3, and your first fully produced episode goes live in week 4.

What do I get after each episode?

The full episode, 6 short-form clips and thumbnails, delivered within hours of going live, plus lead lists, attendance data and ROI reporting.

What do I still own?

Being the voice and face on camera, client relationships and sales, topics and strategic direction, marketing and audience growth, your community and brand, business strategy and positioning, and your own client pricing and packaging.
